Shenzhen Odile Technology Ltd Focus on LED industrial lighting product development, production and sales. At the beginning of the establishment of the company, with LED industrial lighting radiator, (including: LED high bay profiles and phase change radiator, LED floodlight die-casting kit, LED tunnel light profiles and die-casting kits and other radiators and related kits) Production and sales direction. In the radiator, the kit has accumulated valuable experience and advanced production technology. From the beginning of 2008, on the basis of radiator and shell kit, the company expanded the production line, increased the LED industrial lighting finished product development, production and sales business, and further improve the company's products. The company's production plant expanded to 4000 square meters, equipped with advanced optical laboratory (IES test report), light color test, waterproof test, high pressure test a large number of advanced equipment testing equipment, to ensure product quality to provide a strong technical support The The company passed the ISO9001: 2008 quality management system certification, the product through the ETL, DLC, SAA, TUV, CB certification, the products sell well in more than 100 countries and regions! At present, the company unswervingly follow the road of independent research and development to better products and more cost-effective product feedback to our customers support!
